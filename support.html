Support

Getting Help

If you need help using NuetShark, email oralovv26@gmail.com with a short description of the question or issue.

Reporting App Issues

To report an app issue, include your device model, iOS version, app version if available, and the steps that caused the problem. Please do not include sensitive personal information in your message.

Privacy Questions

For privacy questions about NuetShark, email oralovv26@gmail.com. The current released version does not support accounts, authentication, cloud progress sync, analytics, advertising tracking, or production crash reporting.

Deleting Local Data

NuetShark stores exam and study progress locally on your device. You can delete local progress data inside the app from the Data & Progress screen. You can also delete the app from your device to remove app data stored by iOS.

Billing and Purchases

NuetShark does not currently include subscriptions or in-app purchases. If this changes later, App Store billing and refunds will be handled by Apple.
